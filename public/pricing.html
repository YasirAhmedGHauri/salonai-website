Frequently Asked Questions

Can I change plans later?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect immediately, and we'll prorate your billing.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards, JazzCash, EasyPaisa, and bank transfers for annual plans.

Is there a free trial?

Yes! All plans come with a 14-day free trial. No credit card required to start.

What happens if I exceed my booking limit?

We'll notify you when you approach your limit. You can upgrade anytime, or pay a small fee per additional booking.

Can I cancel anytime?

Absolutely. Cancel anytime with no cancellation fees. Your data can be exported before your account closes.
